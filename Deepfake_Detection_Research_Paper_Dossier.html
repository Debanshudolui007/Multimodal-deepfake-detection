1. Abstract

Recent advances in Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and latent diffusion models have made deepfake video and audio synthesis increasingly photorealistic. Traditional unimodal detection pipelines rely primarily on visual artifacts in the spatial domain, which degrade under lossy social media compression, resizing, and adversarial perturbations. In this work, we present an end-to-end tri-modal deepfake detection architecture that jointly models Spatial (RGB), Frequency (FFT + DWT), and Acoustic (MFCC + Mel-Spectrogram) representations.

Our pipeline extracts facial keyframes via RetinaFace and constructs a composite 3-channel frequency representation combining 2D FFT log-magnitude, Daubechies-4 Wavelet (DWT) Low-Low approximation, and high-frequency Wavelet Energy maps (√(LH² + HL² + HH²)). For temporal audio, we extract concatenated 40-dimensional MFCCs and 128-dimensional Mel spectrograms across a fixed temporal window. The system leverages specialized backbones: a ResNeXt-50 (32×4d) for spatial feature extraction (2048-D), a Swin Transformer Tiny with shifted-window self-attention for spectral lattice artifact discovery (768-D), and a 1D CNN + BiLSTM with temporal attention pooling for phonemic and vocal anomaly modeling (512-D). These representations are projected into a shared 512-D latent space and fused via a Multi-Head Transformer Cross-Attention module with dynamic attention pooling and modality dropout (10%).

Trained and validated across 35,089 videos (34.7 GB) from three standard benchmarks (Celeb-DF v2, FaceForensics++ C23, and FakeAVCeleb v1.2), our individual branches achieve validation ROC-AUCs of 96.76% (Spatial), 97.03% (Frequency), and 100.0% (Audio), with the unified cross-attention fusion achieving 95.68% ROC-AUC, 94.19% accuracy, and 96.69% F1-score. Post-hoc Platt scaling is incorporated for well-calibrated posterior probabilities, while Grad-CAM and modality-level attention weight breakdowns provide human-interpretable evidence attribution.

3. Mathematical Formulations & Feature Extraction

3.1 Composite Frequency Tensor (3 × 224 × 224)

For a normalized facial crop converted to grayscale Igray ∈ [0, 1]:

Channel 0 (FFT Magnitude):   F(u, v) = log(1 + |fftshift(ƒ(Igray))|)   [Normalized to 0..1]
Channel 1 (DWT LL Approximation):   LL = DWTdb4(Igray)
Channel 2 (DWT Energy Map):   Edwt = √(LH² + HL² + HH²)

The 3-channel tensor is normalized via dataset statistics: μ = (109.83, 109.83, 109.83), σ = (41.05, 41.05, 41.05).

3.2 Acoustic Feature Representation (168 × 300)

Audio is downmixed to 16 kHz mono PCM. Features comprise 40-dimensional MFCCs concatenated with a 128-dimensional log Mel-frequency spectrogram, padded or trimmed to a 300-frame temporal window (~3.0 seconds).

3.3 Cross-Attention Multi-Head Transformer Fusion

zsp = GELU(LN(Wsp hsp)),   zfreq = GELU(LN(Wfreq hfreq)),   zau = GELU(LN(Wau hau)) ∈ ℝ512
Z = [zsp, zfreq, zau] ∈ ℝB × 3 × 512
Zfused = TransformerEncoder(Z, mask=Mpadding)
α = Softmax(Wp Zfused) = [αsp, αfreq, αau]T,    cfused = ∑m αm Zfused(m)

3.4 Auxiliary Multi-Task Loss

ℒtotal = ℒBCE(ŷfused, y) + 0.3 × ∑m ∈ {sp, freq, au} 𝟙(m valid) · ℒBCE(ŷm, y)

3.5 Post-Hoc Platt Probability Calibration

P(Fake | z) = 1 / (1 + exp(-(a · z + b)))

6. Two-Stage Training Protocol

  • Stage 1 (Frozen Backbone Warmup — Epochs 1 to 10): Unimodal backbones remain frozen. Only projection layers, Transformer Encoder, attention pooling, and classification heads are trained (η = 1.0 × 10-3, AdamW, weight decay 1.0 × 10-4).
  • Stage 2 (End-to-End Fine-Tuning — Epochs 11 to 30): All backbones are unfrozen and trained end-to-end (η = 1.0 × 10-5, Cosine Annealing scheduler, gradient clipping 1.0).
  • Modality Dropout: p = 0.10 during training to ensure the network does not become overly reliant on any single modality.

7. Explainability & Production Deployment

  • Modality Evidence Attribution: The model outputs dynamic softmax weights [αsp, αfreq, αau]. For lip-sync or audio tampering, acoustic weight dominates (αau > 0.70), providing transparent forensic explanation.
  • Spatial Grad-CAM: Activation heatmaps on ResNeXt-50 highlight facial boundaries, mouth blending contours, and artifact locations.
  • Inference Engine & Web Dashboard: Standalone Python API (predictor.py) and interactive Flask dashboard (app.py) enabling real-time video upload and evaluation.
